Green Building Design

Sustainable Infrastructure Concept

Project Overview

This project presents a sustainable building concept aimed at reducing long-term environmental impact while maintaining practical residential comfort. The design emphasizes energy efficiency, passive strategies, and resource-conscious planning.

Major goals included reducing operational energy demand, improving thermal performance, and integrating eco-friendly systems into a buildable and context-aware plan.

Methodology

Concept Planning: Site context, orientation, and climatic behavior were reviewed before finalizing massing and envelope decisions.

Sustainability Features: Natural lighting, ventilation, rainwater reuse potential, and energy-conscious materials were incorporated.

Evaluation: The concept was assessed for functionality, environmental performance, and ease of implementation in local conditions.
